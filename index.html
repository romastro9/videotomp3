FAQ

Good to know.

Are my files uploaded?

No. FFmpeg WebAssembly processes the selected MP4 inside your browser. The media file is not sent to this website’s server.

Is any data stored?

No media, names, settings, history, or personal information are stored. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the session.

Which quality should I choose?

192 kbps is recommended for most audio. Choose 320 kbps when quality matters most, or 128 kbps for a smaller file.

Why can large files be slow?

Conversion uses your device’s memory and processor. Large videos generally work faster on a desktop computer with enough free memory.
